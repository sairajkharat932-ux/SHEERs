WHO WE ARE

Engineering ideas into real-world solutions.

SHEERs is an electrical manufacturing and technology venture focused on developing practical, intelligent and connected electrical products.

From power factor correction to concrete maturity monitoring and AI-powered wind turbine inspection, our vision is to solve real engineering problems through technology.

OUR STORY

Three people. One journey.

From a college classroom to building an electrical technology venture.

AUG 2023
01

Where it began

Sairaj and Harshvardhan met on their very first day of college. Their first lecture after their class teacher was C Programming, taught by Prof. Roopali Palwe.

2023 — 2026
02

Learning & building

With Sairaj bringing a foundation in computer science and coding and Harshvardhan bringing strong hardware knowledge, the three began working together on projects and competitions.

JUL 2026
03

The idea

During Autonation Expo 2026 in Goregaon, Mumbai, Sairaj encountered the Smart Save power factor correction concept and saw an opportunity to build something of their own.

22 AUG 2026
04

SHEERs was born.

Sairaj discussed the idea with Prof. Roopali Palwe and together they began the journey of SHEERs — an electrical technology venture built around innovation, engineering and real-world impact.

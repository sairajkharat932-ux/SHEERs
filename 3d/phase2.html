OUR JOURNEY

How SHEERs was born.

SHEERs began with a friendship, complementary engineering strengths, a teacher who became a mentor, and a simple belief: electrical technology should solve real problems.

2023
THE BEGINNING

Two students. One first meeting.

Sairaj Kharat and Harshvardhan Khade met on their first day of college. Their journey started as classmates and gradually became a partnership built around curiosity, experimentation and engineering.

2023–2026
THE FOUNDATION

Software meets hardware.

Their strengths naturally divided and complemented each other. Sairaj developed software and technology skills, while Harshvardhan focused on hardware, electrical systems and physical implementation. Prof. Roopali Palwe became an important mentor throughout their academic and project journey.

JUL 2026
THE SPARK

An idea found in the real world.

At Autonation Expo 2026 in Goregaon, Mumbai, Sairaj encountered a power-factor correction concept that sparked a bigger question: could they build their own intelligent electrical solution around sensing, control and connected monitoring?

22 AUG 2026
SHEERs IS BORN

From an idea to an electrical technology venture.

The idea was discussed with Prof. Roopali Palwe, bringing together the team's software, hardware and engineering strengths. On 22 August 2026, SHEERs was born with a focus on innovation, engineering and real-world impact.

TODAY
THE JOURNEY CONTINUES

Power smarter. Save better.

SHEERs is developing electrical technology that connects sensing, intelligent control and practical engineering to create solutions for industry and beyond.

THIS IS ONLY THE BEGINNING

Building a smarter electrical future.

OUR STORY

IT STARTED WITH A CLASSROOM.

Three people. One journey.

SHEERs wasn't born in a boardroom. It started in a classroom, through friendship, mentorship, projects and a shared desire to build something real.

2023
THE BEGINNING

First day. First connection.

Sairaj Kharat and Harshvardhan Khade met on their first day of college at Maharashtra Institute of Technology, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

AUG
FIRST LECTURE

PPL — Principles of Programming Language

Their very first lecture was C Programming, taught by Prof. Roopali Palwe. That classroom would eventually become the starting point of a much bigger journey.

2023 — 26
BUILDING TOGETHER

Projects. Competitions. Experience.

Sairaj brought his programming and software foundation. Harshvardhan brought his strong hardware and electronics foundation. Roopali brought mentorship, guidance and strategy. Together they worked on projects and competitions across their college journey.

JUL 2026
THE IDEA

Autonation Expo 2026

During a visit to Autonation Expo 2026 in Goregaon, Mumbai, Sairaj encountered a team demonstrating a power-factor-related device. The thought emerged:

"Why can't we build our own?"
22 AUG
SHEERs

And SHEERs was born.

Sairaj discussed the idea with Prof. Roopali Palwe. On 22 August 2026, the journey that began inside a classroom became something new:

An engineering venture built to solve real-world problems.
